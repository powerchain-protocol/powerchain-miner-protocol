# Community Energy DePIN

## Empowering Communities with DePIN Technology

PowerChain combines renewable-energy operations with Solana settlement and DePIN device
identity to support local energy coordination without treating blockchain data as physical
meter truth.

### Solana Wallet Integration

Connect a Solana-compatible wallet for authorized energy, reward and token settlement.

### Solar Panel Monitoring

Monitor renewable generation and consumption from explicit meter, inverter or EMS
integrations.

### IoT Device Integration

Connect smart meters, Raspberry Pi nodes, ESP32 devices and LoRaWAN/Helium gateways while
maintaining device-specific identities and evidence provenance.

### Energy Analytics

Analyze generation, consumption, storage, verified energy and economic outcomes over time.

### Energy Marketplace

Create reviewable local energy listings and reservations. Physical delivery must be confirmed
by meter evidence before financial settlement.

### Peer-to-Peer Trading

Prepare direct community energy trades with reservation, delivery, reconciliation and
wallet-authorization boundaries.

## Canonical physical-energy flow

```text
LIST
  ↓
REVIEW
  ↓
RESERVE
  ↓
DELIVER
  ↓
METER EVIDENCE
  ↓
RECONCILE
  ↓
SETTLEMENT READY
  ↓
WALLET / PAYMENT AUTHORIZATION
  ↓
SETTLED
```

The typed contracts live in:

```text

```
